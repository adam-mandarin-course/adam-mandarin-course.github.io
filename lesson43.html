我想家了Wǒ xiǎng jiā leI miss home

🏠📞✈️
◀ Previous☰ MenuNext ▶

Dialogue

Him
老婆，我有点儿想家了。
Lǎopó, wǒ yǒudiǎnr xiǎng jiā le.
Honey, I miss home a little.
Her
我知道，你的老家离得很远。
Wǒ zhīdào, nǐ de lǎojiā lí de hěn yuǎn.
I know, your hometown is very far.
Him
嗯，我很想我的爸爸妈妈。
Èn, wǒ hěn xiǎng wǒ de bàba māma.
Yes, I miss my mom and dad a lot.
Her
别难过，我们晚上视频通话吧。
Bié nánguò, wǒmen wǎnshang shìpín tōnghuà ba.
Don't be sad, let's video call tonight.
Him
好。过年我们一起回去，好吗？
Hǎo. Guò nián wǒmen yìqǐ huíqù, hǎo ma?
Okay. Let's go back together for the holiday, okay?
Her
好啊，我去看看机票。
Hǎo a, wǒ qù kànkan jīpiào.
Sure, I'll check plane tickets.
Her
你别担心，我一直陪着你。
Nǐ bié dānxīn, wǒ yìzhí péizhe nǐ.
Don't worry, I'm always here with you.
