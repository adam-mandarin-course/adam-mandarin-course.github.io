Dialogue

Taiyang
你昨天拍的照片，发给我看看。
Nǐ zuótiān pāi de zhàopiàn, fā gěi wǒ kànkan.
The photos you took yesterday, send them to me.
Zozo
好，我用微信发给你。
Hǎo, wǒ yòng wēixìn fā gěi nǐ.
Okay, I'll send them on WeChat.
Taiyang
我还没收到。
Wǒ hái méi shōudào.
I haven't got them yet.
Zozo
可能网速慢，我再发一次。
Kěnéng wǎngsù màn, wǒ zài fā yí cì.
Maybe the internet is slow, I'll send again.
Taiyang
收到了！这张拍得真好。
Shōudào le! Zhè zhāng pāi de zhēn hǎo.
Got it! This one is really well taken.
Zozo
我再发你一个视频。
Wǒ zài fā nǐ yí ge shìpín.
I'll send you a video too.
Taiyang
太好了，我也转发给我家人。
Tài hǎo le, wǒ yě zhuǎnfā gěi wǒ jiārén.
Great, I'll forward it to my family too.
