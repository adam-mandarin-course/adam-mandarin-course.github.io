周末做什么？Zhōu mò zuò shén meWeekend plans

◀ Previous☰ MenuNext ▶

Dialogue

Story: Making plans

Zozo
周末你有空吗？
Zhōumò nǐ yǒu kòng ma?
Are you free this weekend?
Taiyang
有空。你想做什么？
Yǒu kòng. Nǐ xiǎng zuò shénme?
I'm free. What do you want to do?
Zozo
我想出去玩。
Wǒ xiǎng chūqù wán.
I want to go out.
Taiyang
我们去公园，好不好？
Wǒmen qù gōngyuán, hǎo bu hǎo?
Let's go to the park, okay?
Zozo
好啊，这个周末天气很好。
Hǎo a, zhège zhōumò tiānqì hěn hǎo.
Sure, the weather is nice this weekend.
Taiyang
然后我们看电影吧。
Ránhòu wǒmen kàn diànyǐng ba.
And then let's watch a movie.
Zozo
太好了，那就这么定了！
Tài hǎo le, nà jiù zhème dìng le!
Great, then it's settled!

Story: At the park

Zozo
公园的花真漂亮！
Gōngyuán de huā zhēn piàoliàng!
The flowers in the park are so pretty!
Taiyang
我们在这里拍照吧。
Wǒmen zài zhèlǐ pāizhào ba.
Let's take photos here.
Zozo
好，你站在那边。
Hǎo, nǐ zhàn zài nàbiān.
OK, you stand over there.
Taiyang
笑一笑！
Xiàoyīxiào!
Smile!
Zozo
拍得怎么样？
Pāi dé zěnmeyàng?
How did it turn out?
Taiyang
很好看，你笑得很开心。
Hěn hǎokàn, nǐ xiào dé hěn kāixīn.
Very nice, you look very happy.
Zozo
我们走一走吧。
Wǒmen zǒu yī zǒu ba.
Let's take a walk.
Taiyang
好，再去那边坐一会儿。
Hǎo, zài qù nàbiān zuò yīhuìer.
OK, then sit over there for a while.

Story: Coffee break

Taiyang
你累不累？
Nǐ lèibùlèi?
Are you tired?
Zozo
有一点儿，我想喝杯咖啡。
Yǒu yìdiǎnér, wǒ xiǎng hēbēi kāfēi.
A little, I want a cup of coffee.
Taiyang
前面有一家咖啡店。
Qiánmiàn yǒu yījiā kāfēidiàn.
There's a coffee shop ahead.
Zozo
那我们进去休息一下。
Nà wǒmen jìnqù xiūxī yīxià.
Then let's go in and rest a bit.
Taiyang
你想喝什么？
Nǐ xiǎng hē shénme?
What do you want to drink?
Zozo
热咖啡，加一点儿牛奶。
Rèkāfēi, jiā yìdiǎnér niúnǎi.
Hot coffee with a bit of milk.
Taiyang
我也一样。
Wǒ yě yīyàng.
Same for me.
Zozo
今天过得真开心！
Jīntiān guòdé zhēn kāixīn!
Today was really fun!
