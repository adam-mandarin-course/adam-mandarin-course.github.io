周末做什么？ Weekend plans

◀ Previous☰ MenuNext ▶

Dialogue

Her
周末你有空吗？
Zhōumò nǐ yǒu kòng ma?
Are you free this weekend?
Him
有空。你想做什么？
Yǒu kòng. Nǐ xiǎng zuò shénme?
I'm free. What do you want to do?
Her
我想出去玩。
Wǒ xiǎng chūqù wán.
I want to go out.
Him
我们去公园，好不好？
Wǒmen qù gōngyuán, hǎo bu hǎo?
Let's go to the park, okay?
Her
好啊，这个周末天气很好。
Hǎo a, zhège zhōumò tiānqì hěn hǎo.
Sure, the weather is nice this weekend.
Him
然后我们看电影吧。
Ránhòu wǒmen kàn diànyǐng ba.
And then let's watch a movie.
Her
太好了，那就这么定了！
Tài hǎo le, nà jiù zhème dìng le!
Great, then it's settled!
