明年我想……Míng nián wǒ xiǎng ……Next year I want to...

Dialogue

Her
明年你有什么打算？
Míngnián nǐ yǒu shénme dǎsuàn?
What are your plans for next year?
Him
我想继续学中文，说得更好。
Wǒ xiǎng jìxù xué Zhōngwén, shuō de gèng hǎo.
I want to keep studying Chinese and speak better.
Her
真好！我们也可以去旅行。
Zhēn hǎo! Wǒmen yě kěyǐ qù lǚxíng.
Great! We can also travel.
Him
我想去看看你的爸爸妈妈。
Wǒ xiǎng qù kànkan nǐ de bàba māma.
I'd like to go see your mom and dad.
Her
他们一定很高兴。
Tāmen yídìng hěn gāoxìng.
They'll definitely be happy.
Him
到时候我的中文就更好了。
Dào shíhou wǒ de Zhōngwén jiù gèng hǎo le.
By then my Chinese will be better.
Her
对，我们一起去！
Duì, wǒmen yìqǐ qù!
Yes, let's go together!
