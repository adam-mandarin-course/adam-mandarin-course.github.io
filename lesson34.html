今天谁洗碗？Jīn tiān shuí xǐ wǎnWho's washing the dishes?

Dialogue

Zozo
吃完了，今天谁洗碗？
Chī wán le, jīntiān shéi xǐ wǎn?
We're done eating, who washes the dishes today?
Taiyang
昨天是我洗的，今天轮到你了。
Zuótiān shì wǒ xǐ de, jīntiān lúndào nǐ le.
I washed yesterday, it's your turn today.
Zozo
好吧。那你去倒垃圾。
Hǎo ba. Nà nǐ qù dào lājī.
Okay. Then you take out the trash.
Taiyang
没问题。我也扫地，好吗？
Méi wèntí. Wǒ yě sǎo dì, hǎo ma?
No problem. I'll sweep too, okay?
Zozo
太好了，我来洗碗，你拖地。
Tài hǎo le, wǒ lái xǐ wǎn, nǐ tuō dì.
Great, I'll wash, you mop.
Taiyang
好，我们一起做，很快就好。
Hǎo, wǒmen yìqǐ zuò, hěn kuài jiù hǎo.
Okay, we'll do it together, it'll be quick.
Zozo
辛苦了，老公！
Xīnkǔ le, lǎogōng!
Thanks for the effort, hubby!
