我们看什么？Wǒ men kàn shén meWhat shall we watch?

🎬🍿🛋️

Dialogue

Him
今晚我们看什么？
Jīn wǎn wǒmen kàn shénme?
What shall we watch tonight?
Her
看电影吧。有没有好看的？
Kàn diànyǐng ba. Yǒu méiyǒu hǎokàn de?
Let's watch a movie. Is there a good one?
Him
这个很有意思，你看过吗？
Zhège hěn yǒu yìsi, nǐ kànguo ma?
This one is interesting. Have you seen it?
Her
没看过。是什么电影？
Méi kànguo. Shì shénme diànyǐng?
No. What kind of movie is it?
Him
是一个很好笑的电影。
Shì yí ge hěn hǎoxiào de diànyǐng.
It's a very funny movie.
Her
好，那就看这个吧。
Hǎo, nà jiù kàn zhège ba.
Okay, let's watch this one.
Him
太好了，我去拿零食！
Tài hǎo le, wǒ qù ná língshí!
Great, I'll go get snacks!
