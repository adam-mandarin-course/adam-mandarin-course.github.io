Dialogue

Taiyang
老婆，能不能帮我一下？
Lǎopó, néng bu néng bāng wǒ yíxià?
Honey, can you help me a moment?
Zozo
什么事？
Shénme shì?
What is it?
Taiyang
我找不到我的手机。
Wǒ zhǎo bu dào wǒ de shǒujī.
I can't find my phone.
Zozo
你看桌子上面，是不是在那儿？
Nǐ kàn zhuōzi shàngmiàn, shì bu shì zài nàr?
Look on the table. Is it there?
Taiyang
啊，在这儿！谢谢你！
Á, zài zhèr! Xièxie nǐ!
Ah, it's here! Thank you!
Zozo
小事。你每次都找不到。
Xiǎoshì. Nǐ měi cì dōu zhǎo bu dào.
No big deal. You can never find it.
Taiyang
没办法，有你真好。
Méi bànfǎ, yǒu nǐ zhēn hǎo.
Can't help it. It's so good to have you.
