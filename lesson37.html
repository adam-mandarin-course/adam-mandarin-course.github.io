你好，邻居Nǐ hǎo lín jūHello, neighbor

🏢👋🏘️
◀ Previous☰ MenuNext ▶

Dialogue

Shop
你好！你们是新邻居吗？
Nǐ hǎo! Nǐmen shì xīn línjū ma?
Hello! Are you the new neighbors?
Him
对，我们刚搬来，住在三楼。
Duì, wǒmen gāng bān lái, zhù zài sān lóu.
Yes, we just moved in, we live on the third floor.
Shop
欢迎欢迎！我住在你们楼上。
Huānyíng huānyíng! Wǒ zhù zài nǐmen lóushàng.
Welcome! I live upstairs from you.
Her
很高兴认识你。这个小区怎么样？
Hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ. Zhège xiǎoqū zěnmeyàng?
Nice to meet you. How is the neighborhood?
Shop
很安静，邻居们都很好。
Hěn ānjìng, línjūmen dōu hěn hǎo.
Very quiet, the neighbors are all nice.
Shop
有事就来找我。
Yǒu shì jiù lái zhǎo wǒ.
Come find me if you need anything.
Him
谢谢你，我们以后多聊！
Xièxie nǐ, wǒmen yǐhòu duō liáo!
Thank you, let's chat more from now on!
