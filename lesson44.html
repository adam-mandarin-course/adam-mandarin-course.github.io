Dialogue

Zozo
下个月放假，我们去旅行吧！
Xià ge yuè fàng jià, wǒmen qù lǚxíng ba!
Holiday next month, let's go travel!
Taiyang
好啊，你想去哪儿？
Hǎo a, nǐ xiǎng qù nǎr?
Sure, where do you want to go?
Zozo
我想去海边，你呢？
Wǒ xiǎng qù hǎibiān, nǐ ne?
I'd like the seaside, and you?
Taiyang
我也喜欢海边。去几天呢？
Wǒ yě xǐhuan hǎibiān. Qù jǐ tiān ne?
I like the seaside too. How many days?
Zozo
五天吧。我们要提前订酒店。
Wǔ tiān ba. Wǒmen yào tíqián dìng jiǔdiàn.
Five days. We need to book the hotel in advance.
Taiyang
好，我来订，你准备行李。
Hǎo, wǒ lái dìng, nǐ zhǔnbèi xíngli.
Okay, I'll book, you pack the luggage.
Zozo
太期待了！
Tài qīdài le!
I'm so looking forward to it!
