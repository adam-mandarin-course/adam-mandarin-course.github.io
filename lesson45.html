Dialogue

Her
周末你不用来了。
Zhōumò nǐ bú yòng lái le.
You don't need to come on the weekend.
Him
你是什么意思？你不想见我吗？
Nǐ shì shénme yìsi? Nǐ bù xiǎng jiàn wǒ ma?
What do you mean? Don't you want to see me?
Her
不是不是，我是说我去找你。
Bú shì bú shì, wǒ shì shuō wǒ qù zhǎo nǐ.
No no, I mean I'll come to you.
Him
哦，原来是这样，我想错了。
Ó, yuánlái shì zhèyàng, wǒ xiǎng cuò le.
Oh, so that's it, I thought wrong.
Her
是我没说清楚，有点儿误会。
Shì wǒ méi shuō qīngchu, yǒudiǎnr wùhuì.
I didn't say it clearly, a little misunderstanding.
Him
没事，你再解释一下。
Méi shì, nǐ zài jiěshì yíxià.
No problem, explain once more.
Her
好，我换个说法，你就明白了。
Hǎo, wǒ huàn ge shuōfǎ, nǐ jiù míngbai le.
Okay, I'll say it another way and you'll get it.
