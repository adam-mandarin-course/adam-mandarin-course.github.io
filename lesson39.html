Dialogue

Boss Chen
你们好，去哪儿？
Nǐmen hǎo, qù nǎr?
Hello, where to?
Taiyang
师傅，去人民公园，到这儿。
Shīfu, qù Rénmín Gōngyuán, dào zhèr.
Driver, to People's Park, right here on the map.
Zozo
大概要多久？
Dàgài yào duōjiǔ?
About how long will it take?
Boss Chen
二十分钟左右。
Èrshí fēnzhōng zuǒyòu.
About twenty minutes.
Taiyang
我们有点儿着急，能快一点儿吗？
Wǒmen yǒudiǎnr zháojí, néng kuài yìdiǎnr ma?
We're in a bit of a hurry, can you go faster?
Boss Chen
好，前面有点儿堵，我走小路。
Hǎo, qiánmiàn yǒudiǎnr dǔ, wǒ zǒu xiǎolù.
Okay, it's busy ahead, I'll take a side road.
Zozo
就停在前面，用手机支付。谢谢师傅！
Jiù tíng zài qiánmiàn, yòng shǒujī zhīfù. Xièxie shīfu!
Stop up ahead, I'll pay by phone. Thanks driver!
