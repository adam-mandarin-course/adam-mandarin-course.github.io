开玩笑的啦Kāi wán xiào de laJust kidding!

😜🤭😂
◀ Previous☰ MenuNext ▶

Dialogue

Him
老婆，今天的汤是你做的吗？有点儿咸。
Lǎopó, jīntiān de tāng shì nǐ zuò de ma? Yǒudiǎnr xián.
Honey, did you make the soup today? It's a bit salty.
Her
什么？你再说一遍！
Shénme? Nǐ zài shuō yí biàn!
What? Say that again!
Him
哈哈，开玩笑的啦！很好喝！
Hāhā, kāi wánxiào de la! Hěn hǎohē!
Haha, just kidding! It's delicious!
Her
哼，你骗人。
Hēng, nǐ piàn rén.
Hmph, you're lying.
Him
真的，别当真，我是逗你的。
Zhēn de, bié dàngzhēn, wǒ shì dòu nǐ de.
Really, don't take it seriously, I was teasing you.
Her
好啦，不生气了。你笑起来真可爱。
Hǎo la, bù shēngqì le. Nǐ xiào qǐlái zhēn kě'ài.
Okay, I'm not angry. You're cute when you laugh.
Him
谢谢老婆饶了我！
Xièxie lǎopó ráo le wǒ!
Thanks honey for going easy on me!
