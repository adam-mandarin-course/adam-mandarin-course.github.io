Dialogue

Taiyang
老婆，我想你了。
Lǎopó, wǒ xiǎng nǐ le.
Honey, I miss you.
Zozo
真的吗？我也想你。
Zhēn de ma? Wǒ yě xiǎng nǐ.
Really? I miss you too.
Taiyang
你今天真漂亮。
Nǐ jīntiān zhēn piàoliang.
You look so pretty today.
Zozo
谢谢！你也很帅。
Xièxie! Nǐ yě hěn shuài.
Thanks! You're handsome too.
Taiyang
和你在一起，我每天都很开心。
Hé nǐ zài yìqǐ, wǒ měitiān dōu hěn kāixīn.
Being with you, I'm happy every day.
Zozo
我也是。我爱你。
Wǒ yě shì. Wǒ ài nǐ.
Me too. I love you.
Taiyang
我也爱你。来，抱一下！
Wǒ yě ài nǐ. Lái, bào yíxià!
I love you too. Come, a hug!
