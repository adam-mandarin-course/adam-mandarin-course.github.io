我觉得……Wǒ jué de ……I think...

👗💙❤️
◀ Previous☰ MenuNext ▶

Dialogue

Her
这件衣服，蓝色和红色，你觉得哪个好？
Zhè jiàn yīfu, lánsè hé hóngsè, nǐ juéde nǎ ge hǎo?
This dress, blue or red, which do you think is better?
Him
我觉得蓝色比较好看。
Wǒ juéde lánsè bǐjiào hǎokàn.
I think blue looks nicer.
Her
为什么？
Wèishénme?
Why?
Him
蓝色很适合你，也很特别。
Lánsè hěn shìhé nǐ, yě hěn tèbié.
Blue suits you, and it's special.
Her
有道理。可是我也喜欢红色。
Yǒu dàolǐ. Kěshì wǒ yě xǐhuan hóngsè.
Makes sense. But I like red too.
Him
那你觉得呢？
Nà nǐ juéde ne?
Then what do you think?
Her
好，我听你的，买蓝色！
Hǎo, wǒ tīng nǐ de, mǎi lánsè!
Okay, I'll go with you, buying blue!
