Dialogue

Story: Did you make this?

Zozo
这个是你做的吗？
Zhège shì nǐ zuò de ma?
Did you make this?
Taiyang
对，我自己做的！
Duì, wǒ zìjǐ zuò de!
Yes, I made it myself!
Zozo
真的？看起来很好吃。
Zhēn de? Kàn qǐlái hěn hǎochī.
Really? It looks delicious.
Taiyang
你尝一尝，味道怎么样？
Nǐ cháng yi cháng, wèidào zěnmeyàng?
Try it. How does it taste?
Zozo
不错！就是有点儿咸。
Búcuò! Jiùshì yǒudiǎnr xián.
Not bad! Just a bit salty.
Taiyang
哈哈，下次我少放一点儿盐。
Hāhā, xià cì wǒ shǎo fàng yìdiǎnr yán.
Haha, next time I'll use less salt.
Zozo
没关系，我很喜欢。
Méi guānxi, wǒ hěn xǐhuan.
It's okay, I like it a lot.

Story: How I made it

Zozo
你是怎么做的？
Nǐ shì zěnme zuò de?
How did you make it?
Taiyang
先把面条煮五分钟。
Xiān bǎ miàntiáo zhǔ wǔfēnzhōng.
First boil the noodles for five minutes.
Zozo
然后呢？
Ránhòu ne?
And then?
Taiyang
然后放鸡蛋和蔬菜。
Ránhòu fàng jīdàn hé shūcài.
Then add eggs and vegetables.
Zozo
还放别的吗？
Háifàng biéde ma?
Anything else?
Taiyang
最后放一点儿酱油。
Zuìhòu fàng yìdiǎnér jiàngyóu.
Finally a little soy sauce.
Zozo
听起来很简单。
Tīng qǐlái hěn jiǎndān.
Sounds simple.
Taiyang
对，你也可以试试。
Duì, nǐ yě kěyǐ shìshì.
Yes, you can try too.

Story: Your turn to cook

Zozo
我也想自己做一次。
Wǒ yě xiǎng zìjǐ zuò yīcì.
I want to make it myself once too.
Taiyang
好，我教你。
Hǎo, wǒjiào nǐ.
OK, I'll teach you.
Zozo
要放多少盐？
Yàofàng duōshǎo yán?
How much salt?
Taiyang
少放一点儿就行。
Shǎofàng yìdiǎnér jiùxíng.
Just a little is fine.
Zozo
哎呀，水太多了！
Āiyā, shuǐtàiduō le!
Oops, too much water!
Taiyang
没关系，再加一点儿面条。
Méiguānxì, zài jiā yìdiǎnér miàntiáo.
It's okay, add a few more noodles.
Zozo
这次味道怎么样？
Zhècì wèidào zěnmeyàng?
How does it taste this time?
Taiyang
比我做的还好吃！
Bǐ wǒ zuò de hái hǎochī!
Even better than mine!
