Dialogue

Zozo
喂，你在哪儿？
Wèi, nǐ zài nǎr?
Hello, where are you?
Taiyang
我在路上，马上到家。
Wǒ zài lùshang, mǎshàng dào jiā.
I'm on the way, almost home.
Zozo
你什么时候到？我们等你吃饭。
Nǐ shénme shíhou dào? Wǒmen děng nǐ chī fàn.
When do you get here? We're waiting to eat with you.
Taiyang
有点儿堵车，还要十分钟。
Yǒudiǎnr dǔ chē, hái yào shí fēnzhōng.
A bit of traffic, ten more minutes.
Zozo
好，你快一点儿，我等你。
Hǎo, nǐ kuài yìdiǎnr, wǒ děng nǐ.
Okay, a bit faster, I'll wait for you.
Taiyang
别着急，我马上就到。
Bié zháojí, wǒ mǎshàng jiù dào.
Don't worry, I'll be there soon.
Zozo
好，路上小心！
Hǎo, lùshang xiǎoxīn!
Okay, be careful on the road!
