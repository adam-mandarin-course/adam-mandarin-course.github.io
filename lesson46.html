Dialogue

Taiyang
老婆，我跟你说，今天太好笑了！
Lǎopó, wǒ gēn nǐ shuō, jīntiān tài hǎoxiào le!
Honey, let me tell you, today was so funny!
Zozo
怎么了？快说快说。
Zěnme le? Kuài shuō kuài shuō.
What happened? Tell me quick.
Taiyang
我先去超市，然后在门口差点儿摔倒。
Wǒ xiān qù chāoshì, ránhòu zài ménkǒu chàdiǎnr shuāidǎo.
First I went to the supermarket, then almost fell at the door.
Zozo
啊？吓我一跳！有没有事？
Á? Xià wǒ yí tiào! Yǒu méiyǒu shì?
Ah? You scared me! Are you okay?
Taiyang
没事，还好有一个阿姨拉住了我。
Méi shì, hái hǎo yǒu yí ge āyí lā zhù le wǒ.
I'm fine, luckily an auntie caught me.
Taiyang
结果她的菜掉了一地，最后我们一起捡。
Jiéguǒ tā de cài diào le yí dì, zuìhòu wǒmen yìqǐ jiǎn.
As a result her groceries fell everywhere, and finally we picked them up together.
Zozo
哈哈，笑死我了！
Hāhā, xiào sǐ wǒ le!
Haha, I'm dying of laughter!
