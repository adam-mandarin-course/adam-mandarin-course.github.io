Dialogue

Him
老婆，我回来了！
Lǎopó, wǒ huílái le!
Honey, I'm home!
Her
回来啦！今天过得怎么样？
Huílái la! Jīntiān guò de zěnmeyàng?
You're back! How was your day?
Him
有点儿累，今天很忙。你呢？
Yǒudiǎnr lèi, jīntiān hěn máng. Nǐ ne?
A bit tired, it was very busy today. And you?
Her
我还好。你吃饭了吗？
Wǒ hái hǎo. Nǐ chī fàn le ma?
I'm okay. Have you eaten?
Him
还没有。晚上想吃什么？
Hái méiyǒu. Wǎnshang xiǎng chī shénme?
Not yet. What do you want to eat tonight?
Her
我想吃面条。
Wǒ xiǎng chī miàntiáo.
I want noodles.
Him
好，我来做！
Hǎo, wǒ lái zuò!
Okay, I'll make them!

Words

今天   jīntiān
today
怎么样   zěnmeyàng
how (is it)?
忙   máng
busy
累   lèi
tired
有点儿   yǒudiǎnr
a little, kind of
工作   gōngzuò
work
回来   huílái
come back / get home
还好   hái hǎo
okay, not bad
你呢   nǐ ne
and you?
吃饭   chī fàn
to eat (a meal)
还没有   hái méiyǒu
not yet
晚上   wǎnshang
evening, tonight
面条   miàntiáo
noodles
我来做   wǒ lái zuò
I'll cook it / I'll do it
