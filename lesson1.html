今天过得怎么样？Jīn tiān guò dé zěn me yàngHow was your day?

◀ Previous☰ MenuNext ▶

Dialogue

Story: Coming home

Taiyang
老婆，我回来了！
Lǎopó, wǒ huílái le!
Honey, I'm home!
Zozo
回来啦！今天过得怎么样？
Huílái la! Jīntiān guò de zěnmeyàng?
You're back! How was your day?
Taiyang
有点儿累，今天很忙。你呢？
Yǒudiǎnr lèi, jīntiān hěn máng. Nǐ ne?
A bit tired, it was very busy today. And you?
Zozo
我还好。你吃饭了吗？
Wǒ hái hǎo. Nǐ chī fàn le ma?
I'm okay. Have you eaten?
Taiyang
还没有。晚上想吃什么？
Hái méiyǒu. Wǎnshang xiǎng chī shénme?
Not yet. What do you want to eat tonight?
Zozo
我想吃面条。
Wǒ xiǎng chī miàntiáo.
I want noodles.
Taiyang
好，我来做！
Hǎo, wǒ lái zuò!
Okay, I'll make them!

Story: After dinner

Taiyang
面条好吃吗？
Miàntiáo hǎochī ma?
Do you like the noodles? (Are they tasty?)
Zozo
很好吃！老公，你真厉害。
Hěn hǎochī! Lǎogōng, nǐ zhēn lìhai.
Delicious! Hubby, you're amazing.
Taiyang
哈哈，谢谢老婆。
Hāha, xièxie lǎopó.
Haha, thanks honey.
Zozo
明天你忙吗？
Míngtiān nǐ máng ma?
Are you busy tomorrow?
Taiyang
明天不忙。怎么了？
Míngtiān bù máng. Zěnme le?
Not busy tomorrow. What's up?
Zozo
我们去看电影，好不好？
Wǒmen qù kàn diànyǐng, hǎo bù hǎo?
Let's go watch a movie, okay?
Taiyang
好啊！我们几点去？
Hǎo a! Wǒmen jǐ diǎn qù?
Sure! What time shall we go?
Zozo
晚上七点。
Wǎnshang qī diǎn.
Seven in the evening.

Story: At the cinema

Zozo
我们到了！你想喝什么？
Wǒmen dào le! Nǐ xiǎng hē shénme?
We're here! What do you want to drink?
Taiyang
我想喝可乐。你呢？
Wǒ xiǎng hē kělè. Nǐ ne?
I want a cola. And you?
Zozo
我喝茶。爆米花你要吗？
Wǒ hē chá. Bàomǐhuā nǐ yào ma?
I'll have tea. Do you want popcorn?
Taiyang
要，我们买一个大的。
Yào, wǒmen mǎi yí ge dà de.
Yes, let's buy a big one.
Zozo
电影好看吗？
Diànyǐng hǎokàn ma?
Was the movie good?
Taiyang
很好看！我很喜欢。
Hěn hǎokàn! Wǒ hěn xǐhuan.
Very good! I liked it a lot.
Zozo
我们明天还来，好不好？
Wǒmen míngtiān hái lái, hǎo bù hǎo?
Shall we come again tomorrow?
Taiyang
好啊，不过明天我有点儿累。
Hǎo a, búguò míngtiān wǒ yǒudiǎnr lèi.
Sure, but tomorrow I'm a bit tired.
