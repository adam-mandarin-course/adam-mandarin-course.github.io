生日快乐！Shēng rì kuài lèHappy birthday!

🎂🎁🕯️
◀ Previous☰ MenuNext ▶

Dialogue

Him
老婆，生日快乐！
Lǎopó, shēngrì kuàilè!
Honey, happy birthday!
Her
谢谢！哇，有蛋糕！
Xièxie! Wā, yǒu dàngāo!
Thank you! Wow, there's a cake!
Him
还有礼物，给你一个惊喜。
Hái yǒu lǐwù, gěi nǐ yí ge jīngxǐ.
And a gift, a surprise for you.
Her
真的吗？我很喜欢！
Zhēn de ma? Wǒ hěn xǐhuan!
Really? I love it!
Him
来，先许愿，再吹蜡烛。
Lái, xiān xǔ yuàn, zài chuī làzhú.
Come, make a wish first, then blow out the candles.
Her
好，我许好了。
Hǎo, wǒ xǔ hǎo le.
Okay, I've made my wish.
Him
祝你身体健康，每天开心！
Zhù nǐ shēntǐ jiànkāng, měitiān kāixīn!
Wishing you good health and happiness every day!
