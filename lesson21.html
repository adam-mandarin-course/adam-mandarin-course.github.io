对不起，别生气Duì bù qǐ bié shēng qìSorry, don't be angry

🙇💐🤗

Dialogue

Her
你怎么又忘了？我有点儿生气。
Nǐ zěnme yòu wàng le? Wǒ yǒudiǎnr shēngqì.
How did you forget again? I'm a bit angry.
Him
对不起，老婆，是我的错。
Duìbuqǐ, lǎopó, shì wǒ de cuò.
I'm sorry, honey, it's my fault.
Him
下次我一定记得。
Xià cì wǒ yídìng jìde.
Next time I'll definitely remember.
Her
你每次都这么说。
Nǐ měi cì dōu zhème shuō.
You say that every time.
Him
这次是真的。你别生气了，好不好？
Zhè cì shì zhēn de. Nǐ bié shēngqì le, hǎo bu hǎo?
This time it's real. Don't be angry, okay?
Her
好吧，没关系，我原谅你。
Hǎo ba, méi guānxi, wǒ yuánliàng nǐ.
Okay, it's fine, I forgive you.
Him
谢谢老婆！来，抱抱！
Xièxie lǎopó! Lái, bàobao!
Thanks honey! Come, a hug!
