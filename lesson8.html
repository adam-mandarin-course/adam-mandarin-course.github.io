我们怎么去？Wǒ men zěn me qùGetting around

◀ Previous☰ MenuNext ▶

Dialogue

Story: How do we get there?

Zozo
我们怎么去超市？
Wǒmen zěnme qù chāoshì?
How do we get to the supermarket?
Taiyang
不远，走路十分钟就到。
Bù yuǎn, zǒu lù shí fēnzhōng jiù dào.
Not far. Ten minutes on foot.
Zozo
太热了，打车吧。
Tài rè le, dǎ chē ba.
It's too hot, let's take a taxi.
Taiyang
好，我现在叫车。
Hǎo, wǒ xiànzài jiào chē.
Okay, I'll call one now.
Zozo
要多久？
Yào duōjiǔ?
How long will it take?
Taiyang
开车五分钟。我们出发吧！
Kāi chē wǔ fēnzhōng. Wǒmen chūfā ba!
Five minutes by car. Let's go!
Zozo
好，走吧！
Hǎo, zǒu ba!
Okay, let's go!

Story: In the taxi

Taiyang
师傅，去前面的超市。
Shīfù, qù qiánmiàn de chāoshì.
Driver, to the supermarket ahead.
Driver
好的，系好安全带。
Hǎo de, jìhǎo ānquándài.
OK, buckle up.
Zozo
路上车多吗？
Lùshàngchē duō ma?
Is there a lot of traffic?
Driver
今天不多，很快就到。
Jīntiān bù duō, hěnkuài jiù dào.
Not today, we'll be there soon.
Taiyang
在前面路口停就行。
Zài qiánmiàn lùkǒu tíngjiùxíng.
Stopping at the next intersection is fine.
Driver
好，一共十五块。
Hǎo, yīgòng shíwǔkuài.
OK, fifteen yuan in total.
Zozo
我来付钱。
Wǒlái fùqián.
I'll pay.
Taiyang
谢谢老婆！
Xièxiè lǎopó!
Thanks, honey!

Story: Taking the bus home

Zozo
回去我们坐公交车吧。
Huíqù wǒmen zuò gōngjiāochē ba.
Let's take the bus back.
Taiyang
公交站在哪儿？
Gōngjiāozhàn zài nǎér?
Where's the bus stop?
Zozo
就在前面，走两分钟。
Jiù zài qiánmiàn, zǒu liǎngfēnzhōng.
Right ahead, two minutes' walk.
Taiyang
几路车到我们家？
Jǐlù chēdào wǒmen jiā?
Which bus goes to our home?
Zozo
看，二十路来了！
Kàn, èrshí lùlái le!
Look, bus 20 is here!
Taiyang
快跑，别错过了！
Kuài pǎo, biécuòguò le!
Run, don't miss it!
Zozo
哈哈，赶上了。
Hāhā, gǎnshàng le.
Haha, we caught it.
Taiyang
今天走了好多路。
Jīntiān zǒu le hǎoduō lù.
We walked a lot today.
