Dialogue

Zozo
我们怎么去超市？
Wǒmen zěnme qù chāoshì?
How do we get to the supermarket?
Taiyang
不远，走路十分钟就到。
Bù yuǎn, zǒu lù shí fēnzhōng jiù dào.
Not far. Ten minutes on foot.
Zozo
太热了，打车吧。
Tài rè le, dǎ chē ba.
It's too hot, let's take a taxi.
Taiyang
好，我现在叫车。
Hǎo, wǒ xiànzài jiào chē.
Okay, I'll call one now.
Zozo
要多久？
Yào duōjiǔ?
How long will it take?
Taiyang
开车五分钟。我们出发吧！
Kāi chē wǔ fēnzhōng. Wǒmen chūfā ba!
Five minutes by car. Let's go!
Zozo
好，走吧！
Hǎo, zǒu ba!
Okay, let's go!

Words

怎么去   zěnme qù
how to get there
走路   zǒu lù
to walk
打车   dǎ chē
to take a taxi
开车   kāi chē
to drive
远   yuǎn
far
近   jìn
near
多久   duōjiǔ
how long
分钟   fēnzhōng
minutes
到   dào
to arrive
叫车   jiào chē
to call a ride
出发   chūfā
to set off
