我迷路了Wǒ mí lù leI'm lost

🧭🗺️🚶
◀ Previous☰ MenuNext ▶

Dialogue

Him
请问，地铁站怎么走？我迷路了。
Qǐngwèn, dìtiězhàn zěnme zǒu? Wǒ mílù le.
Excuse me, how do I get to the subway station? I'm lost.
Shop
往前走，到红绿灯往左拐。
Wǎng qián zǒu, dào hónglǜdēng wǎng zuǒ guǎi.
Go straight, turn left at the traffic light.
Him
往左拐，然后呢？
Wǎng zuǒ guǎi, ránhòu ne?
Turn left, and then?
Shop
然后一直走，地铁站在你的右边。
Ránhòu yìzhí zǒu, dìtiězhàn zài nǐ de yòubiān.
Then keep going, the station is on your right.
Him
远吗？
Yuǎn ma?
Is it far?
Shop
不远，走路五分钟。
Bù yuǎn, zǒu lù wǔ fēnzhōng.
Not far, five minutes on foot.
Him
太谢谢你了！
Tài xièxie nǐ le!
Thank you so much!
