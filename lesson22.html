Dialogue

Her
你昨天做了什么？
Nǐ zuótiān zuò le shénme?
What did you do yesterday?
Him
我和朋友去了公园。
Wǒ hé péngyou qù le gōngyuán.
I went to the park with a friend.
Her
你们在公园做了什么？
Nǐmen zài gōngyuán zuò le shénme?
What did you do in the park?
Him
我们拍了很多照片，还吃了冰激凌。
Wǒmen pāi le hěn duō zhàopiàn, hái chī le bīngjīlíng.
We took lots of photos and ate ice cream.
Her
你几点回来的？
Nǐ jǐ diǎn huílái de?
What time did you come back?
Him
很晚，十点多。我太累了。
Hěn wǎn, shí diǎn duō. Wǒ tài lèi le.
Very late, after ten. I was so tired.
Her
难怪你今天起不来。
Nánguài nǐ jīntiān qǐ bu lái.
No wonder you can't get up today.
