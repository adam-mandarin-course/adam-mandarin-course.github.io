Dialogue

Taiyang
你好，我咳嗽三天了，嗓子也疼。
Nǐ hǎo, wǒ késou sān tiān le, sǎngzi yě téng.
Hello, I've had a cough for three days, and a sore throat.
Boss Chen
发烧吗？
Fāshāo ma?
Any fever?
Taiyang
没有发烧。
Méiyǒu fāshāo.
No fever.
Boss Chen
那吃这个药，一天三次，饭后吃。
Nà chī zhège yào, yì tiān sān cì, fàn hòu chī.
Then take this medicine, three times a day, after meals.
Taiyang
需要多喝水吗？
Xūyào duō hē shuǐ ma?
Do I need to drink lots of water?
Boss Chen
要，多休息，好得快。
Yào, duō xiūxi, hǎo de kuài.
Yes, and rest more, you'll get better faster.
Boss Chen
如果更严重，就去看医生。
Rúguǒ gèng yánzhòng, jiù qù kàn yīshēng.
If it gets worse, see a doctor.
