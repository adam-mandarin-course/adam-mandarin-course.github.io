多少钱？Duō shǎo qiánPrices and buying

◀ Previous☰ MenuNext ▶

Dialogue

Story: Asking the price

Taiyang
老板，这个多少钱？
Lǎobǎn, zhège duōshao qián?
Boss, how much is this?
Vendor
二十块。
Èrshí kuài.
Twenty yuan.
Taiyang
太贵了，能不能便宜一点儿？
Tài guì le, néng bu néng piányi yìdiǎnr?
Too expensive. Can you make it cheaper?
Vendor
那十八块吧。
Nà shíbā kuài ba.
Okay, eighteen then.
Taiyang
好，我要三个，一共多少钱？
Hǎo, wǒ yào sān ge, yígòng duōshao qián?
Okay, I'll take three. How much altogether?
Vendor
一共五十四块。
Yígòng wǔshísì kuài.
Fifty-four altogether.
Taiyang
给你钱，谢谢！
Gěi nǐ qián, xièxie!
Here you go, thanks!

Story: Fruit market

Taiyang
这个苹果怎么卖？
Zhège píngguǒ zěnme mài?
How do you sell these apples?
Vendor
八块一斤。
Bākuài yījīn.
Eight yuan a jin.
Taiyang
有点儿贵，七块行不行？
Yǒudiǎnér guì, qīkuài xíngbùxíng?
A bit pricey, would seven do?
Vendor
不行不行，七块五吧。
Bùxíng bùxíng, qīkuài wǔ ba.
No no, seven fifty.
Taiyang
好吧，给我两斤。
Hǎo ba, gěi wǒ liǎngjīn.
Alright, give me two jin.
Vendor
还要别的吗？
Háiyào biéde ma?
Anything else?
Taiyang
再来一斤香蕉。
Zài lái yījīn xiāngjiāo.
One more jin of bananas.
Vendor
一共三十一块。
Yīgòng sānshí yīkuài.
Thirty-one in total.

Story: Paying

Vendor
你扫码还是给现金？
Nǐ sǎomǎ háishì gěi xiànjīn?
Scan the code or cash?
Taiyang
我扫码，可以吗？
Wǒ sǎomǎ, kěyǐ ma?
I'll scan, okay?
Vendor
可以，扫这里。
Kěyǐ, sǎo zhèlǐ.
Yes, scan here.
Taiyang
好了，你看一下。
Hǎo le, nǐ kàn yīxià.
Done, take a look.
Vendor
收到了，谢谢！
Shōudào le, xièxiè!
Received, thanks!
Vendor
要不要袋子？
Yào búyào dàizi?
Do you want a bag?
Taiyang
不用了，我有包。
Bùyòng le, wǒ yǒu bāo.
No need, I have a bag.
Vendor
好，慢走，欢迎再来！
Hǎo, mànzǒu, huānyíng zài lái!
OK, take care, welcome back!
