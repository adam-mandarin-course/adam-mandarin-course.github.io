Dialogue

Taiyang
老板，这个多少钱？
Lǎobǎn, zhège duōshao qián?
Boss, how much is this?
Boss Chen
二十块。
Èrshí kuài.
Twenty yuan.
Taiyang
太贵了，能不能便宜一点儿？
Tài guì le, néng bu néng piányi yìdiǎnr?
Too expensive. Can you make it cheaper?
Boss Chen
那十八块吧。
Nà shíbā kuài ba.
Okay, eighteen then.
Taiyang
好，我要三个，一共多少钱？
Hǎo, wǒ yào sān ge, yígòng duōshao qián?
Okay, I'll take three. How much altogether?
Boss Chen
一共五十四块。
Yígòng wǔshísì kuài.
Fifty-four altogether.
Taiyang
给你钱，谢谢！
Gěi nǐ qián, xièxie!
Here you go, thanks!

Words

多少钱   duōshao qián
how much money
块   kuài

二十   èrshí

十八   shíbā

便宜   piányi
cheap
能不能   néng bu néng

一共   yígòng

三个   sān ge
