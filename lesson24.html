你家人好吗？Nǐ jiā rén hǎo maHow is your family?

👨‍👩‍👧📹🏠
◀ Previous☰ MenuNext ▶

Dialogue

Him
你爸爸妈妈身体好吗？
Nǐ bàba māma shēntǐ hǎo ma?
Are your mom and dad in good health?
Her
他们都很好，谢谢。你的家人呢？
Tāmen dōu hěn hǎo, xièxie. Nǐ de jiārén ne?
They're both well, thanks. And your family?
Him
我家人也很好，他们很想你。
Wǒ jiārén yě hěn hǎo, tāmen hěn xiǎng nǐ.
My family is fine too, they miss you.
Her
我也想他们。我们今晚视频吧。
Wǒ yě xiǎng tāmen. Wǒmen jīn wǎn shìpín ba.
I miss them too. Let's video call tonight.
Him
好，我给你哥哥姐姐问好。
Hǎo, wǒ gěi nǐ gēge jiějie wèn hǎo.
Okay, I'll say hello to your brother and sister.
Her
他们放假回家，也会很高兴。
Tāmen fàng jià huí jiā, yě huì hěn gāoxìng.
They're home for the holiday, they'll be happy too.
Him
太好了，一家人在一起。
Tài hǎo le, yì jiā rén zài yìqǐ.
Wonderful, the whole family together.
