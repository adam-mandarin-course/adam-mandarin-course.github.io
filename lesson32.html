Dialogue

Zozo
我今天在路上看到了一只大熊猫！
Wǒ jīntiān zài lùshang kàndào le yì zhī dà xióngmāo!
I saw a giant panda on the road today!
Taiyang
真的假的？怎么会？
Zhēn de jiǎ de? Zěnme huì?
Really? No way! How come?
Zozo
是一个人穿的衣服，在做广告。
Shì yí ge rén chuān de yīfu, zài zuò guǎnggào.
It was a person in a costume, doing an ad.
Taiyang
哈哈，原来如此！然后呢？
Hāhā, yuánlái rúcǐ! Ránhòu ne?
Haha, I see! And then?
Zozo
然后我们拍了照，太好玩了。
Ránhòu wǒmen pāi le zhào, tài hǎowán le.
Then we took a photo, so much fun.
Taiyang
没想到！给我看看！
Méi xiǎngdào! Gěi wǒ kànkan!
Didn't expect that! Show me!
Zozo
来，你看，我也觉得很可爱。
Lái, nǐ kàn, wǒ yě juéde hěn kě'ài.
Here, look, I think it's cute too.
