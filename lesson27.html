Dialogue

Zozo
客厅的灯坏了，不亮了。
Kètīng de dēng huài le, bú liàng le.
The living room light is broken, it won't turn on.
Taiyang
可能是灯泡坏了，我来换。
Kěnéng shì dēngpào huài le, wǒ lái huàn.
Maybe the bulb is gone. I'll change it.
Zozo
你有梯子吗？
Nǐ yǒu tīzi ma?
Do you have a ladder?
Taiyang
有，在门口。
Yǒu, zài ménkǒu.
Yes, by the door.
Zozo
小心一点儿！
Xiǎoxīn yìdiǎnr!
Be careful!
Taiyang
好了，搞定！你看，亮了。
Hǎo le, gǎodìng! Nǐ kàn, liàng le.
Done, sorted! Look, it's on.
Zozo
你真棒，我不用找人修了。
Nǐ zhēn bàng, wǒ bú yòng zhǎo rén xiū le.
You're great, no need to find someone to fix it.
