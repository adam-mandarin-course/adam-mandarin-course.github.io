都可以，听你的Dōu kě yǐ tīng nǐ deAnything's fine, up to you

🍲🍜🤝

Dialogue

Him
今晚吃什么？你来决定吧。
Jīn wǎn chī shénme? Nǐ lái juédìng ba.
What for dinner tonight? You decide.
Her
都可以，听你的。
Dōu kěyǐ, tīng nǐ de.
Anything is fine, up to you.
Him
你每次都说随便，这次我们商量一下。
Nǐ měi cì dōu shuō suíbiàn, zhè cì wǒmen shāngliang yíxià.
You always say whatever, let's discuss this time.
Her
好，我想吃火锅，你想吃面条。
Hǎo, wǒ xiǎng chī huǒguō, nǐ xiǎng chī miàntiáo.
Okay, I want hotpot, you want noodles.
Him
这样吧，今天火锅，明天面条。
Zhèyàng ba, jīntiān huǒguō, míngtiān miàntiáo.
How about this, hotpot today, noodles tomorrow.
Her
好主意，我们轮流决定。
Hǎo zhǔyi, wǒmen lúnliú juédìng.
Good idea, we'll take turns deciding.
Him
我同意，各让一步！
Wǒ tóngyì, gè ràng yí bù!
I agree, each gives a little!
