Dialogue

Him
服务员，请给我们菜单。
Fúwùyuán, qǐng gěi wǒmen càidān.
Waiter, a menu please.
Shop
好的，这是菜单。需要推荐吗？
Hǎo de, zhè shì càidān. Xūyào tuījiàn ma?
Sure, here's the menu. Want a recommendation?
Her
有什么招牌菜？
Yǒu shénme zhāopai cài?
What's your signature dish?
Shop
我们的鱼很好吃，也有点儿辣。
Wǒmen de yú hěn hǎochī, yě yǒudiǎnr là.
Our fish is great, a bit spicy.
Him
那就要这个，不要太辣。再来两碗米饭。
Nà jiù yào zhège, bú yào tài là. Zài lái liǎng wǎn mǐfàn.
We'll take it, not too spicy. And two bowls of rice.
Shop
好的，够吃吗？
Hǎo de, gòu chī ma?
Okay, is that enough?
Her
够了，先这些吧。
Gòu le, xiān zhèxiē ba.
Enough, these for now.
