能便宜一点儿吗？Néng pián yi yì diǎn ér maCan it be a bit cheaper?

🏷️🤝🧺
◀ Previous☰ MenuNext ▶

Dialogue

Him
老板，这个多少钱？
Lǎobǎn, zhège duōshao qián?
Boss, how much is this?
Shop
八十块。
Bāshí kuài.
Eighty yuan.
Him
太贵了。能便宜一点儿吗？
Tài guì le. Néng piányi yìdiǎnr ma?
Too expensive. Can it be a bit cheaper?
Shop
最低六十，不能再低了。
Zuì dī liùshí, bù néng zài dī le.
Sixty is my lowest, can't go lower.
Him
我买两个，打折吧。
Wǒ mǎi liǎng ge, dǎ zhé ba.
I'll buy two, give a discount.
Shop
好吧，两个一百，再送你一个小的。
Hǎo ba, liǎng ge yì bǎi, zài sòng nǐ yí ge xiǎo de.
Okay, one hundred for two, plus a small one free.
Him
好，成交！
Hǎo, chéngjiāo!
Good, deal!

Words

便宜   piányi
cheap
贵   guì
expensive
太贵了   tài guì le
too expensive
最低   zuì dī
lowest
打折   dǎ zhé
discount
算了   suàn le
forget it
再看看   zài kànkan
look around more
好吧   hǎo ba
all right
两个   liǎng ge
two of them
送   sòng
give as a gift
成交   chéngjiāo
deal
