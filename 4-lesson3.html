我们去买东西 Let's go shopping

◀ Previous☰ MenuNext ▶

Dialogue

Him
我们什么时候去买东西？
Wǒmen shénme shíhou qù mǎi dōngxi?
When are we going shopping?
Her
明天下午去吧。
Míngtiān xiàwǔ qù ba.
Let's go tomorrow afternoon.
Him
好。我们要买什么？
Hǎo. Wǒmen yào mǎi shénme?
Okay. What do we need to buy?
Her
要买肉、蔬菜和水果。
Yào mǎi ròu, shūcài hé shuǐguǒ.
We need meat, vegetables and fruit.
Him
去超市还是去市场？
Qù chāoshì háishi qù shìchǎng?
The supermarket or the market?
Her
去超市吧，我们一起去。
Qù chāoshì ba, wǒmen yìqǐ qù.
The supermarket. Let's go together.
Him
好，那就明天下午一起去！
Hǎo, nà jiù míngtiān xiàwǔ yìqǐ qù!
Okay, then tomorrow afternoon, together!
