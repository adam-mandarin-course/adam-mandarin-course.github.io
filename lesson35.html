早上好，晚安Zǎo shàng hǎo wǎn ānGood morning, good night

🌅😴🌙
◀ Previous☰ MenuNext ▶

Dialogue

Him
早上好，老婆！睡得好吗？
Zǎoshang hǎo, lǎopó! Shuì de hǎo ma?
Good morning, honey! Did you sleep well?
Her
睡得很好，我还做了一个梦。
Shuì de hěn hǎo, wǒ hái zuò le yí ge mèng.
Slept well, and I had a dream too.
Him
你梦到了什么？
Nǐ mèngdào le shénme?
What did you dream about?
Her
我梦到我们去了海边。
Wǒ mèngdào wǒmen qù le hǎibiān.
I dreamt we went to the beach.
Him
真好。闹钟响了，你还想睡吗？
Zhēn hǎo. Nàozhōng xiǎng le, nǐ hái xiǎng shuì ma?
Nice. The alarm's ringing, still sleepy?
Her
想，再睡一会儿吧。
Xiǎng, zài shuì yíhuìr ba.
Yes, let me sleep a little more.
Him
好，晚安，好梦。
Hǎo, wǎn'ān, hǎo mèng.
Okay, good night, sweet dreams.
