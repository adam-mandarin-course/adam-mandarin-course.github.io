谢谢你为我做的Xiè xiè nǐ wèi wǒ zuò deThanks for all you do

Dialogue

Taiyang
老婆，谢谢你为我做的一切。
Lǎopó, xièxie nǐ wèi wǒ zuò de yíqiè.
Honey, thank you for everything you do for me.
Zozo
怎么突然这么说？
Zěnme tūrán zhème shuō?
Why are you suddenly saying this?
Taiyang
你每天照顾我，真的很辛苦。
Nǐ měitiān zhàogu wǒ, zhēn de hěn xīnkǔ.
You take care of me every day, it's really hard work.
Zozo
这是应该的，我们是一家人。
Zhè shì yīnggāi de, wǒmen shì yì jiā rén.
It's only natural, we're family.
Taiyang
遇到你，我觉得很幸福。
Yùdào nǐ, wǒ juéde hěn xìngfú.
Meeting you, I feel so blessed.
Zozo
我也是。我们要永远在一起。
Wǒ yě shì. Wǒmen yào yǒngyuǎn zài yìqǐ.
Me too. We'll be together forever.
Taiyang
一言为定！
Yì yán wéi dìng!
It's a promise!
