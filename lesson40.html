去公园走走Qù gōng yuán zǒu zǒuA walk in the park

🌸🌳🪑
◀ Previous☰ MenuNext ▶

Dialogue

Him
今天天气真好，我们去公园散步吧。
Jīntiān tiānqì zhēn hǎo, wǒmen qù gōngyuán sàn bù ba.
The weather is great, let's walk in the park.
Her
好啊！这里空气真新鲜。
Hǎo a! Zhèlǐ kōngqì zhēn xīnxiān.
Yes! The air here is so fresh.
Him
你看，花都开了。
Nǐ kàn, huā dōu kāi le.
Look, the flowers have all bloomed.
Her
真美！我们拍张照吧。
Zhēn měi! Wǒmen pāi zhāng zhào ba.
So beautiful! Let's take a photo.
Him
好，来，笑一个！
Hǎo, lái, xiào yí ge!
Okay, smile!
Her
我走累了，我们坐一会儿吧。
Wǒ zǒu lèi le, wǒmen zuò yíhuìr ba.
I'm tired from walking, let's sit a while.
Him
那边有长椅，我去买冰水。
Nàbiān yǒu chángyǐ, wǒ qù mǎi bīng shuǐ.
There's a bench over there, I'll go buy cold water.
