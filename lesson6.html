Dialogue

Taiyang
老板，我想要这个。
Lǎobǎn, wǒ xiǎng yào zhège.
Boss, I'd like this one.
Boss Chen
好的，要几个？
Hǎo de, yào jǐ ge?
Okay, how many?
Taiyang
要两个。有没有大一点儿的？
Yào liǎng ge. Yǒu méiyǒu dà yìdiǎnr de?
Two. Do you have a bigger one?
Boss Chen
有，你看一下这个。
Yǒu, nǐ kàn yíxià zhège.
Yes, take a look at this one.
Taiyang
可以，就要这个，谢谢！
Kěyǐ, jiù yào zhège, xièxie!
Good, I'll take this one, thanks!
Boss Chen
不客气。
Bú kèqi.
You're welcome.
Taiyang
再来一个小的，可以吗？
Zài lái yí ge xiǎo de, kěyǐ ma?
One more small one, please?
Boss Chen
好的，没问题。
Hǎo de, méi wèntí.
Okay, no problem.

Words

do you have...?
