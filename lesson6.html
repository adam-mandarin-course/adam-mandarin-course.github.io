我想要这个Wǒ xiǎng yào zhè geAsking for things

◀ Previous☰ MenuNext ▶

Dialogue

Story: At the market stall

Taiyang
老板，我想要这个。
Lǎobǎn, wǒ xiǎng yào zhège.
Boss, I'd like this one.
Shopkeeper
好的，要几个？
Hǎo de, yào jǐ ge?
Okay, how many?
Taiyang
要两个。有没有大一点儿的？
Yào liǎng ge. Yǒu méiyǒu dà yìdiǎnr de?
Two. Do you have a bigger one?
Shopkeeper
有，你看一下这个。
Yǒu, nǐ kàn yíxià zhège.
Yes, take a look at this one.
Taiyang
可以，就要这个，谢谢！
Kěyǐ, jiù yào zhège, xièxie!
Good, I'll take this one, thanks!
Shopkeeper
不客气。
Bú kèqi.
You're welcome.
Taiyang
再来一个小的，可以吗？
Zài lái yí ge xiǎo de, kěyǐ ma?
One more small one, please?
Shopkeeper
好的，没问题。
Hǎo de, méi wèntí.
Okay, no problem.

Story: Clothes shop

Zozo
我想看看这件衣服。
Wǒ xiǎng kànkàn zhèjiàn yīfú.
I'd like to look at this piece of clothing.
Shopkeeper
好的，你想要什么颜色？
Hǎo de, nǐ xiǎngyào shénme yánsè?
OK, what color do you want?
Zozo
有没有红色的？
Yǒuméiyǒu hóngsè de?
Do you have a red one?
Shopkeeper
有，你要大号还是小号？
Yǒu, nǐ yào dà hào háishì xiǎohào?
Yes, large or small?
Zozo
小号。我可以试一下吗？
Xiǎohào. wǒ kěyǐ shìyīxià ma?
Small. Can I try it on?
Shopkeeper
可以，试衣间在那边。
Kěyǐ, shìyījiān zài nàbiān.
Sure, the fitting room is over there.
Zozo
怎么样？
Zěnmeyàng?
How is it?
Taiyang
很好看，就买这件吧。
Hěn hǎokàn, jiù mǎi zhèjiàn ba.
Looks great, let's buy this one.

Story: A gift for her

Shopkeeper
欢迎光临！你想看什么？
Huānyíngguānglín! nǐ xiǎng kàn shénme?
Welcome! What would you like to see?
Taiyang
我想给老婆买一个礼物。
Wǒ xiǎng gěi lǎopó mǎi yígè lǐwù.
I want to buy a gift for my wife.
Shopkeeper
这个杯子很可爱，你看看。
Zhège bēizi hěn kěài, nǐ kànkàn.
This cup is cute, take a look.
Taiyang
嗯，有别的颜色吗？
Ń, yǒu biéde yánsè ma?
Hm, any other colors?
Shopkeeper
有蓝色和粉色的。
Yǒu lánsè hé fěnsè de.
Blue and pink ones.
Taiyang
她喜欢粉色，要粉色的。
Tā xǐhuān fěnsè, yào fěnsè de.
She likes pink, I'll take pink.
Shopkeeper
需要包起来吗？
Xūyào bāo qǐlái ma?
Do you need it wrapped?
Taiyang
好的，谢谢你！
Hǎo de, xièxiè nǐ!
Yes please, thank you!
