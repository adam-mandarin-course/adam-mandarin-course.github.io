Dialogue

Zozo
你明天要早点儿起床。
Nǐ míngtiān yào zǎodiǎnr qǐchuáng.
You need to get up early tomorrow.
Taiyang
对不起，我没听懂。请再说一遍。
Duìbuqǐ, wǒ méi tīngdǒng. Qǐng zài shuō yí biàn.
Sorry, I didn't understand. Please say it again.
Zozo
好，我说慢一点儿。明天，早点儿，起床。
Hǎo, wǒ shuō màn yìdiǎnr. Míngtiān, zǎodiǎnr, qǐchuáng.
Okay, slower. Tomorrow, earlier, get up.
Taiyang
哦，听懂了！“起床”是什么意思？
Ó, tīngdǒng le! “Qǐchuáng” shì shénme yìsi?
Oh, I understand! What does 起床 mean?
Zozo
就是不睡觉了，从床上起来。
Jiù shì bú shuì jiào le, cóng chuáng shàng qǐlái.
It means to stop sleeping and get out of bed.
Taiyang
我的中文还不太好。
Wǒ de Zhōngwén hái bú tài hǎo.
My Chinese is still not very good.
Zozo
慢慢来，你很棒，加油！
Mànman lái, nǐ hěn bàng, jiāyóu!
Take it slowly, you're great, keep going!
