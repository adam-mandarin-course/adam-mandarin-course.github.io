Dialogue

Taiyang
老婆，你今天心情不好吗？
Lǎopó, nǐ jīntiān xīnqíng bù hǎo ma?
Honey, are you in a bad mood today?
Zozo
有点儿累，也有点儿压力。
Yǒudiǎnr lèi, yě yǒudiǎnr yālì.
A bit tired, and a bit stressed.
Taiyang
我们聊聊吧，我听你说。
Wǒmen liáoliao ba, wǒ tīng nǐ shuō.
Let's talk, I'm listening.
Zozo
有时候我觉得你不理解我。
Yǒu shíhou wǒ juéde nǐ bù lǐjiě wǒ.
Sometimes I feel you don't understand me.
Taiyang
对不起，我以后会多听你说。
Duìbuqǐ, wǒ yǐhòu huì duō tīng nǐ shuō.
Sorry, I'll listen to you more from now on.
Taiyang
我陪你出去走走，放松一下，好吗？
Wǒ péi nǐ chūqù zǒuzou, fàngsōng yíxià, hǎo ma?
I'll go for a walk with you to relax, okay?
Zozo
好，我现在好多了，谢谢你。
Hǎo, wǒ xiànzài hǎo duō le, xièxie nǐ.
Okay, I feel much better now, thank you.
