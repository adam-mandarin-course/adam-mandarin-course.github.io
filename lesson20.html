买单Mǎi dānThe bill, please

🧾😋💳
◀ Previous☰ MenuNext ▶

Dialogue

Him
这条鱼真好吃！你吃饱了吗？
Zhè tiáo yú zhēn hǎochī! Nǐ chī bǎo le ma?
This fish is so good! Are you full?
Her
吃饱了，真不错。
Chī bǎo le, zhēn búcuò.
I'm full, it was really good.
Him
服务员，买单。
Fúwùyuán, mǎi dān.
Waiter, the bill please.
Shop
一共一百二十块。
Yígòng yì bǎi èrshí kuài.
One hundred twenty in total.
Her
这次我请客，你上次请的。
Zhè cì wǒ qǐng kè, nǐ shàng cì qǐng de.
My treat this time, you paid last time.
Him
好，那下次我请。
Hǎo, nà xià cì wǒ qǐng.
Okay, next time is mine.
Her
我来扫码付钱。谢谢招待！
Wǒ lái sǎo mǎ fù qián. Xièxie zhāodài!
I'll scan and pay. Thanks for treating me!
