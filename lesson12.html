Dialogue

Zozo
现在几点了？
Xiànzài jǐ diǎn le?
What time is it now?
Taiyang
现在七点半。
Xiànzài qī diǎn bàn.
It's seven thirty.
Zozo
啊，我们几点要出门？
Á, wǒmen jǐ diǎn yào chū mén?
Oh, what time do we have to leave?
Taiyang
八点出门，还来得及。
Bā diǎn chū mén, hái láidejí.
We leave at eight, there's still time.
Zozo
那我去准备一下，你等我十分钟。
Nà wǒ qù zhǔnbèi yíxià, nǐ děng wǒ shí fēnzhōng.
Then I'll get ready. Wait for me ten minutes.
Taiyang
好，不着急。我在门口等你。
Hǎo, bù zháojí. Wǒ zài ménkǒu děng nǐ.
Okay, no rush. I'll wait at the door.
Zozo
好，我马上就好！
Hǎo, wǒ mǎshàng jiù hǎo!
Okay, I'll be ready in a moment!
