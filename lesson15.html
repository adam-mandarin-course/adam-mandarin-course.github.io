Dialogue

Taiyang
你想喝点儿什么？
Nǐ xiǎng hē diǎnr shénme?
What would you like to drink?
Zozo
我想喝茶，你呢？
Wǒ xiǎng hē chá, nǐ ne?
I'd like tea. And you?
Taiyang
我喝咖啡。你要热的还是冰的？
Wǒ hē kāfēi. Nǐ yào rè de háishi bīng de?
I'll have coffee. Hot or iced for you?
Zozo
热的。不要加糖。
Rè de. Bú yào jiā táng.
Hot. No sugar.
Taiyang
好，我也不加糖。
Hǎo, wǒ yě bù jiā táng.
Okay, no sugar for me either.
Zozo
再给我一点儿牛奶，可以吗？
Zài gěi wǒ yìdiǎnr niúnǎi, kěyǐ ma?
Can I have a little more milk?
Taiyang
没问题，马上来！
Méi wèntí, mǎshàng lái!
No problem, coming right up!
