Dialogue

Story: Hungry at home

Zozo
我好饿！晚上吃什么？
Wǒ hǎo è! Wǎnshang chī shénme?
I'm so hungry! What's for dinner?
Taiyang
你想吃什么？
Nǐ xiǎng chī shénme?
What do you want to eat?
Zozo
我想吃鸡肉和米饭。
Wǒ xiǎng chī jīròu hé mǐfàn.
I want chicken and rice.
Taiyang
要不要喝汤？
Yào bu yào hē tāng?
Do you want soup?
Zozo
好啊！不要太辣。
Hǎo a! Bú yào tài là.
Sure! Not too spicy.
Taiyang
没问题，我来做饭。
Méi wèntí, wǒ lái zuò fàn.
No problem, I'll cook.
Zozo
太好了，老公最好了！
Tài hǎo le, lǎogōng zuì hǎo le!
Great, you're the best, honey!

Story: Cooking together

Zozo
我来帮你吧。
Wǒlái bāng nǐ ba.
I'll help you.
Taiyang
好，你洗菜，我切肉。
Hǎo, nǐ xǐcài, wǒ qièròu.
OK, you wash the vegetables, I'll cut the meat.
Zozo
鸡肉要多少？
Jīròu yào duōshǎo?
How much chicken do we need?
Taiyang
一块就够了。
Yīkuài jiùgòu le.
One piece is enough.
Zozo
汤里放什么？
Tānglǐ fàng shénme?
What goes in the soup?
Taiyang
放蔬菜和一点儿盐。
Fàng shūcài hé yìdiǎnér yán.
Vegetables and a little salt.
Zozo
好香啊！
Hǎoxiāng a!
It smells so good!
Taiyang
再等十分钟。
Zài děng shífēnzhōng.
Wait ten more minutes.

Story: Dinner time

Taiyang
可以吃饭了！
Kěyǐ chīfàn le!
Time to eat!
Zozo
哇，好多菜！
Wa, hǎoduō cài!
Wow, so many dishes!
Taiyang
尝一尝这个汤。
Chángyīcháng zhège tāng.
Try this soup.
Zozo
很好喝！不太辣。
Hěn hǎohē! bùtàilà.
Very tasty! Not too spicy.
Taiyang
你还要米饭吗？
Nǐ háiyào mǐfàn ma?
Do you want more rice?
Zozo
再来一点儿，谢谢。
Zài lái yìdiǎnér, xièxiè.
A little more, thanks.
Taiyang
吃完以后我洗碗。
Chī wán yǐhòu wǒ xǐwǎn.
After we eat, I'll wash the dishes.
Zozo
你真好，我爱你。
Nǐ zhēn hǎo, wǒàinǐ.
You're so kind, I love you.
