Dialogue

Taiyang
今天天气怎么样？
Jīntiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng?
How's the weather today?
Zozo
天气预报说下午下雨。
Tiānqì yùbào shuō xiàwǔ xià yǔ.
The forecast says rain this afternoon.
Taiyang
那我带伞吧。
Nà wǒ dài sǎn ba.
Then I'll bring an umbrella.
Zozo
外面还有风，好像有点儿冷。
Wàimiàn hái yǒu fēng, hǎoxiàng yǒudiǎnr lěng.
It's windy out there too, it seems a bit cold.
Taiyang
那我多穿点儿，带外套。
Nà wǒ duō chuān diǎnr, dài wàitào.
Then I'll wear more and take a jacket.
Zozo
对，明天就是晴天了。
Duì, míngtiān jiù shì qíngtiān le.
Right, tomorrow will be sunny.
Taiyang
太好了，明天我们出去玩！
Tài hǎo le, míngtiān wǒmen chūqù wán!
Great, let's go out tomorrow!
