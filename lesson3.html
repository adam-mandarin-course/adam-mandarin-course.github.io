我们去买东西Wǒ men qù mǎi dōng xīLet's go shopping

◀ Previous☰ MenuNext ▶

Dialogue

Story: Planning to shop

Taiyang
我们什么时候去买东西？
Wǒmen shénme shíhou qù mǎi dōngxi?
When are we going shopping?
Zozo
明天下午去吧。
Míngtiān xiàwǔ qù ba.
Let's go tomorrow afternoon.
Taiyang
好。我们要买什么？
Hǎo. Wǒmen yào mǎi shénme?
Okay. What do we need to buy?
Zozo
要买肉、蔬菜和水果。
Yào mǎi ròu, shūcài hé shuǐguǒ.
We need meat, vegetables and fruit.
Taiyang
去超市还是去市场？
Qù chāoshì háishi qù shìchǎng?
The supermarket or the market?
Zozo
去超市吧，我们一起去。
Qù chāoshì ba, wǒmen yìqǐ qù.
The supermarket. Let's go together.
Taiyang
好，那就明天下午一起去！
Hǎo, nà jiù míngtiān xiàwǔ yìqǐ qù!
Okay, then tomorrow afternoon, together!

Story: In the supermarket

Zozo
我们先买蔬菜吧。
Wǒmen xiānmǎi shūcài ba.
Let's buy vegetables first.
Taiyang
好，这些西红柿怎么样？
Hǎo, zhèxiē xīhóngshì zěnmeyàng?
OK, how about these tomatoes?
Zozo
看起来很新鲜。
Kànqǐlái hěn xīnxiān.
They look fresh.
Taiyang
肉在哪里？
Ròu zài nǎlǐ?
Where's the meat?
Zozo
在前面，往右走。
Zài qiánmiàn, wǎngyòu zǒu.
Up ahead, turn right.
Taiyang
水果你想要什么？
Shuǐguǒ nǐ xiǎngyào shénme?
What fruit do you want?
Zozo
我想买苹果和香蕉。
Wǒ xiǎng mǎi píngguǒ hé xiāngjiāo.
I want apples and bananas.
Taiyang
好，放进车里。
Hǎo, fàngjìn chēlǐ.
OK, put them in the cart.

Story: Checkout

Zozo
我们买完了吗？
Wǒmen mǎi wán le ma?
Are we done buying?
Taiyang
还要牛奶和鸡蛋。
Háiyào niúnǎi hé jīdàn.
We still need milk and eggs.
Zozo
对，差点儿忘了。
Duì, chàdiǎnér wàng le.
Right, I almost forgot.
Taiyang
排队的人很多。
Páiduì de rén hěnduō.
There's a long line.
Zozo
没关系，我们等一会儿。
Méiguānxì, wǒmen děng yīhuìer.
It's fine, we'll wait a little.
Taiyang
今天一共花了多少钱？
Jīntiān yīgòng huā le duōshǎo qián?
How much did we spend today?
Zozo
三百块。东西有点儿多。
Sānbǎikuài. dōngxī yǒudiǎnér duō.
Three hundred. A lot of stuff.
Taiyang
我来拿，你拿轻的。
Wǒlái ná, nǐ ná qīng de.
I'll carry, you take the light ones.
