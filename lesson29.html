来我家吃饭吧Lái wǒ jiā chī fàn baCome to our place for dinner

🏡🍲🥂
◀ Previous☰ MenuNext ▶

Dialogue

Him
这个周六来我家吃饭吧！
Zhège zhōuliù lái wǒ jiā chī fàn ba!
Come to our place for dinner this Saturday!
Shop
周六晚上？我有空！
Zhōuliù wǎnshang? Wǒ yǒu kòng!
Saturday evening? I'm free!
Her
太好了，我们做你爱吃的菜。
Tài hǎo le, wǒmen zuò nǐ ài chī de cài.
Great, we'll cook your favorite dishes.
Shop
我带点儿水果去吧。
Wǒ dài diǎnr shuǐguǒ qù ba.
I'll bring some fruit.
Him
别客气，不用带东西。
Bié kèqi, bú yòng dài dōngxi.
Don't be polite, no need to bring anything.
Shop
那好，一言为定！
Nà hǎo, yì yán wéi dìng!
All right, it's a promise!
Her
我们很期待！
Wǒmen hěn qīdài!
We're looking forward to it!
